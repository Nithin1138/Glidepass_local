## 1. POLICY OVERVIEW

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") defines prohibited and permitted uses of LANpad. Violation of this AUP may result in:

- Termination of your LANpad license
- Deletion of your data
- Reporting to law enforcement
- Civil or criminal legal action
- Academic or employment disciplinary action

---

## 2. PROHIBITED USES

### 2.1 ❌ Academic Integrity Violations

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Bypass proctoring software, exam monitoring, or exam-integrity tools (e.g., Respondus, ProctorU, Proctorio)
- Cheat on exams, quizzes, assessments, or academic tests
- Circumvent keyboard locks or paste-block restrictions on exam platforms
- Violate your institution's academic honor code or integrity policies
- Help another person cheat or bypass academic security

**Why?**
- Using LANpad during exams may constitute academic fraud
- You may face disciplinary action (grade penalty, suspension, expulsion)
- In some jurisdictions, exam cheating is a criminal offense
- Academic institutions can prosecute or seek civil damages

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad to paste code during a locked-down coding exam
- ❌ Using LANpad to bypass HackerRank's paste restrictions during a timed assessment
- ❌ Using LANpad during online exams with proctoring enabled
- ❌ Sharing LANpad with classmates to help them cheat

---

### 2.2 ❌ Unauthorized Computer Access

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Gain unauthorized access to computer systems or networks (violates CFAA in US, ITA § 66 in India)
- Bypass authentication systems, firewalls, or security controls
- Access data on computers/systems you do not own or have explicit written permission to access
- Modify, delete, or disrupt computer systems
- Escalate privileges or impersonate other users

**Why?**
- Unauthorized computer access is a criminal offense in most jurisdictions
- US: Computer Fraud & Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 USC § 1030 (federal crime)
- India: Information Technology Act § 66 (up to 3 years imprisonment)
- You may face both criminal prosecution and civil liability

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad to bypass login screens on systems you don't own
- ❌ Using LANpad to access banking websites with someone else's credentials
- ❌ Using LANpad to bypass corporate security on your employer's network
- ❌ Using LANpad to access government or military computer systems

---

### 2.3 ❌ Credential Theft & Phishing

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Steal, phish, or harvest credentials (usernames, passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, 2FA codes)
- Perform credential stuffing (testing stolen credentials against multiple sites)
- Conduct brute-force attacks on authentication systems
- Impersonate others or commit identity fraud
- Facilitate money laundering or financial fraud

**Why?**
- Credential theft is a federal crime (CFAA § 1030(a)(6), US identity theft laws)
- India: ITA § 66C (identity theft, up to 3 years imprisonment)
- Victims may sue you for damages
- Banks and institutions will pursue prosecution

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad to test stolen passwords against email accounts
- ❌ Using LANpad to inject login credentials to access someone else's bank account
- ❌ Using LANpad to perform phishing attacks
- ❌ Using LANpad to automate credential harvesting

---

### 2.4 ❌ Privacy & Security Violations

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Capture, intercept, or sniff other users' clipboard data on shared networks
- Eavesdrop on network traffic or perform man-in-the-middle (MITM) attacks
- Deploy keystroke loggers or malware
- Exploit vulnerabilities in LANpad or other software for unauthorized gain
- Participate in DDoS attacks or network disruption

**Why?**
- Wiretapping/eavesdropping is a federal crime (US Wiretap Act, state laws)
- India: ITA § 66 (unauthorized access, § 43 civil liability)
- Victims may sue for privacy invasion
- Civil and criminal penalties apply

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad on a public Wi-Fi network to capture other users' clipboard data
- ❌ Setting up a rogue Wi-Fi hotspot to intercept LANpad traffic
- ❌ Using LANpad to deploy malware or keyloggers

---

### 2.5 ❌ Institutional Policy Violations

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Violate your workplace, school, government agency, or institutional policies
- Bypass corporate security controls, DLP systems, or information governance
- Circumvent data loss prevention (DLP) measures
- Access restricted resources against institutional authorization
- Use LANpad on restricted networks without permission

**Why?**
- Violating institutional policies can result in disciplinary action (suspension, termination, expulsion)
- May constitute theft of trade secrets or intellectual property
- Could violate employment agreements or student codes of conduct
- Developer is not liable for institutional discipline

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad at work to bypass corporate paste restrictions on restricted sites
- ❌ Using LANpad to access confidential company documents without authorization
- ❌ Using LANpad on a government network without security clearance
- ❌ Using LANpad to circumvent school's internet filtering policies

---

### 2.6 ❌ Harassment, Abuse & Illegal Activity

**You MUST NOT use LANpad to:**

- Harass, threaten, abuse, defame, or stalk others
- Distribute malware, viruses, worms, or other malicious code
- Commit fraud, extortion, or blackmail
- Facilitate illegal drug sales or other criminal enterprise
- Violate export controls, sanctions, or embargoes
- Violate any local, state, national, or international laws

**Why?**
- These are criminal offenses in virtually all jurisdictions
- Victims may sue for damages
- Federal and state criminal penalties apply
- Developer will cooperate with law enforcement

**Example Violations:**
- ❌ Using LANpad to automate harassment messages
- ❌ Using LANpad to distribute stolen software or copyrighted content
- ❌ Using LANpad to facilitate illegal sales

## 3. PERMITTED USES

### 3.1 ✅ Accessibility

You MAY use LANpad to:

- **Assist users with disabilities** who cannot use standard clipboard or keyboard input
- **Comply with accessibility standards** (ADA, Section 508, AODA, Equality Act 2010)
- **Enable equal access** for users with motor, visual, or cognitive disabilities

**Requirements:**
- You must have explicit authorization from the relevant institution (school, employer)
- You must use LANpad **only for accessibility purposes**, not to bypass security
- You should document the accessibility need and obtain proper approval

**Example Permitted Use:**
- ✅ A student with motor disabilities using LANpad to type during exams (with institutional accommodation approval)
- ✅ An employee using LANpad on authorized systems due to disability
- ✅ A developer testing keyboard injection for accessibility research (on test systems only)

---

### 3.2 ✅ Personal Productivity (Authorized Systems Only)

You MAY use LANpad to:

- Transfer text between your own personal devices (laptop + personal phone)
- Move code snippets between your own systems
- Sync notes or credentials across your own devices (within your LAN)
- Use LANpad on systems you own, control, or have explicit written permission to use

**Requirements:**
- Both devices must be yours or under your authorized control
- You must have consent from the system owner/administrator (if not yours)
- Use must comply with institutional policies

**Example Permitted Uses:**
- ✅ Moving code snippets from your phone to your laptop
- ✅ Syncing credentials across your personal devices
- ✅ Using LANpad on your home network
- ✅ Using LANpad on a dev machine you own

---

### 3.3 ✅ Development, Testing & Research

You MAY use LANpad to:

- Test LANpad functionality on your own systems
- Conduct accessibility or security research (with ethical oversight)
- Contribute to LANpad development
- Debug issues on test/sandbox systems

**Requirements:**
- Testing must be on systems you own, control, or have explicit permission for
- Research must comply with institutional ethics review (IRB, if applicable)
- You must not test on production systems without authorization
- You must document your testing and share findings responsibly

**Example Permitted Uses:**
- ✅ Testing keyboard injection on your personal Windows VM
- ✅ Researching LANpad security on lab systems
- ✅ Contributing security patches to LANpad development

## 9. JURISDICTIONAL NOTES

### India (ITA 2000)
- § 66: Unauthorized computer access (3 years imprisonment, ₹500K fine)
- § 66B: Dishonesty / receiving stolen computer resources (3 years, ₹1M fine)
- § 66C: Identity theft (3 years, ₹1M fine)
- § 43: Civil liability for unauthorized access (up to ₹10M damages)

### United States (CFAA)
- § 1030(a)(2): Unauthorized access (civil liability)
- § 1030(a)(5): Damage to computer (federal crime, up to 10 years)
- § 1030(a)(6): Trafficking in passwords/credentials (federal crime, up to 15 years)

### European Union (GDPR, ePrivacy Directive)
- Unauthorized access to data = data protection violation + potential criminal liability
- Phishing/credential theft = fraud + potential criminal liability
