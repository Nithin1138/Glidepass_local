Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 19, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how LANpad collects, uses, stores, and protects your data. LANpad is designed with privacy-by-default principles: data is processed locally on your devices and is never transmitted to external servers.

By using LANpad, you consent to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use LANpad.

2. WHAT DATA DOES LANpad PROCESS?

2.1 Data You Provide

Data Purpose Retained?
Clipboard contents Transfer between devices Temporarily (during session)
Text you type Inject into applications Temporarily (during session)
Workspace/Resource metadata Snippet titles, languages, tags, and timestamps Indefinitely on database (for shared resources)
Configuration data Store preferences, license info Indefinitely (in local storage)

2.2 Technical Data Processed Automatically

Data Purpose Retained?
Local IP address Generate QR code for mobile pairing Session-only
Device hostname Display in UI for identification Session-only
Viewer Fingerprint Hash Track unique resource views (IP + User-Agent SHA256 hash) 30 days (auto-expired)
Session token Authenticate pairing connection Session-only

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email, or personal identity
  • ❌ GPS location or device location
  • ❌ Browsing history or web pages visited
  • ❌ Keystroke logs (outside the active session)
  • ❌ Third-party account credentials

3. WHERE IS MY DATA STORED?

3.1 Local-Only Processing

LANpad processes data only on your local network. Your clipboard data never leaves your local Wi-Fi network, and no external servers store your personal data.

Note: data is transmitted via HTTP (unencrypted) over your local Wi-Fi. Ensure your Wi-Fi network is password-protected and uses WPA3 encryption.

4. HOW IS MY DATA USED?

We process data to enable text transfer between your devices, support users with disabilities via keystroke injection, and authenticate pairing between devices.

We NEVER sell or share your clipboard data, nor do we track or profile you.

4A. UNIQUE VIEWER TRACKING

To measure content reach for creators on the discovery platform without compromising privacy, we compute a hashed fingerprint of your IP address and User-Agent (using SHA-256). This hash is stored for 30 days to prevent duplicate view/copy counts. The raw IP address and User-Agent are discarded immediately. You can opt out of this tracking by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.
