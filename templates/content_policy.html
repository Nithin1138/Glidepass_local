Content Policy

Last Updated: June 19, 2026

1. ALLOWED CONTENT

LANpad allows the publishing and sharing of code snippets, workspace configurations, notes, documentation links, and utility files intended for legitimate development, educational, or productivity purposes.

2. PROHIBITED CONTENT

You must not upload, share, or publish any resource that contains:

  • Malware & Exploits: Viruses, spyware, Trojans, logic bombs, ransomware, or scripts designed to gain unauthorized access to other systems.
  • Copyright Infringement: Source code, proprietary assets, textbook materials, or private proprietary software uploaded without authorization.
  • Abuse & Harassment: Content that threatens, defames, bullies, or promotes discrimination against individuals or groups.
  • Spam & Phishing: Unrelated advertising, repetitive messages, obfuscated malicious URLs, or forms designed to harvest user credentials.
  • Adult/Sensitive Content: Content that is sexually explicit, graphic, violent, or otherwise inappropriate for educational and professional workspaces.

3. ENFORCEMENT & REMOVALS

Reports submitted by users are processed daily. If any content is found to violate this Content Policy, we reserve the right to remove it immediately without notice and suspend or terminate the creator's account or license keys.

4. APPEALS

If your content was removed in error, you may file an appeal by contacting us at appeals@example.com with the resource ID and justification.
